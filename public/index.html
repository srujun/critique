How to use Critique

Critique tracks whose turn it is on every open PR you're involved in, like Google's attention set.

Reading the dashboard

  • Needs your attention means it's your move. Waiting on others means it's someone else's.
  • Dot color: your turn, your turn soon (a reviewer's comment is still in its debounce window), not your turn.
  • A filled dot means activity you haven't opened yet. A hollow one means you've opened the PR since.
  • Reviewer names are colored by their review: approved, changes requested, commented, pending. Bold means it's that person's turn.
  • The badge says what's next. For reviews: New, Updated (new commits or replies since your review; follow the blue link to see only what changed), Re-review. For your PRs: Fix, Respond, Merge, Stuck (auto-merge stalled).

How the turn moves

  • A reviewer submits a review: the author's turn. "Comment" reviews wait for the debounce first.
  • The author re-requests review: the reviewer's turn.
  • The author pushes after your comments or change requests: your turn again, once the push settles for the debounce period. You can turn this off in Settings.
  • Someone @mentions or assigns you: your turn until you respond.
  • Drafts, PRs set to auto-merge, and closed PRs are nobody's turn. On any other PR, if nobody else has the turn (say no reviewers are requested yet), the author does.

Actions

  • ✕ dismisses a PR into Dismissed. It gets a blue dot on new activity. Use Restore to bring it back.
  • ⋯ → Not my turn takes you out of the attention set until someone else reviews, comments or pushes, then puts you back. It also mutes a repo or a whole owner (unmute in Settings).
  • ◐ switches the theme: System (follows your OS), Light, or Dark.
  • While a Critique tab is open, you get a browser notification when a PR becomes your turn. The tab title and icon show the count.

Reviewing in Critique

  • ± on a PR, or its blue "since your review" link, opens the review in a side panel. Drag its edge to resize; Esc or Back closes it. Cmd/Ctrl-click opens it in a new tab instead.
  • Whole PR shows everything; Since your review shows only what changed after your last review. Or compare any two versions with the dropdowns. If the branch was rebased in between, it compares each file the PR touches, so the base branch's changes stay out.
  • Files are grouped into Changes, then Tests, then Generated (from the repo's linguist-generated rules in .gitattributes, plus common patterns like lockfiles). Tree/Flat switches the list layout; a folder or section checkbox marks everything in it viewed.
  • Check Viewed on a file to collapse it. If the file changes later, it un-checks itself and is marked Changed since you viewed.
  • GitHub comment threads show on their lines. Threads waiting on your reply are red, here and on the dashboard. Reply on GitHub.
